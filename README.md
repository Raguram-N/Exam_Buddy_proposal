# ExamBuddy AI: Voice-First Exam Learning Assistant

**Author:** Raguram Narayanaswamy

---

## 1. Context & Problem

Millions of aspirants preparing for TNPSC, UPSC, SSC, Banking, and Railway examinations spend significant time reading books, searching PDFs, watching videos, and revising notes.

While AI tools can answer questions, they are designed for general-purpose knowledge and often do not provide syllabus-focused, source-backed responses aligned with competitive examinations.

Additionally, students lose valuable learning time during commuting, walking, exercising, and other daily activities where traditional study methods are not practical.

---

## 2. Core Innovation

ExamBuddy AI proposes a **voice-first learning experience** where aspirants can interact with an AI tutor through natural conversations.

Instead of searching through multiple books and resources, students simply ask:

- "Summarize the Gandhiyan phase of independence."

- "Summarize the Sector of macro economics"

- "Generate 10 MCQs from this topic."

- "Create revision notes."

The system responds instantly using trusted educational sources and exam-oriented explanations.

## 4. Trusted Knowledge Architecture

Unlike generic AI systems, ExamBuddy AI is designed to utilize verified educational sources such as:

* NCERT Textbooks
* Tamil Nadu Samacheer Books
* Previous Year Question Papers
* Official Answer Keys
* Government Publications

The objective is to provide exam-oriented, source-backed learning assistance. So that we can differ from other generalized AI.
